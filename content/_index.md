### Welcome to Works@Scale 
Today's enterprises are hindered by accumulated technical debt. The prospect of reengineering a monolithic software base is often judged as too risky, too difficult, and just not urgent right now.

**Works@Scale** assists enterprises in reengineering legacy software using low-risk methods. Our goal is to replace the current monolith with smaller, loosely coupled systems that are:
* **Easier to maintain**
* **More resilient to failure**
* **Flexible for new business requirements**
